Background & Business Problem

A large property management company receives hundreds of maintenance requests every week via email and WhatsApp. The portfolio includes residential apartment complexes, commercial office buildings, and government-occupied buildings. The operations team currently reads each message manually, identifies the building, apartment, issue type, urgency, and responsible vendor, and then creates a work order in their existing ERP system.

The client wants a pilot that reduces manual triage time and automates as much of the end-to-end flow as possible. The system should handle everything from the initial request until the required intervention is successfully completed — including contacting the supplier to arrange the maintenance date and managing all communication with the tenant throughout the process.

The goal is to achieve the lowest possible operational cost with the highest possible outcome reliability.

The client expects something live in production and handling real cases within 3 weeks.

Example Messages & Exchanges

The following examples represent the kind of messages the operations team receives. Your system should be able to handle all of them.

These examples are illustrative of a type of interaction, not representative of the full range of interaction types or complexities the system will encounter in practice.

Message 1 — WhatsApp · Residential Building
"Good morning, the elevator in block B has stopped again and there is an elderly lady on the 5th floor who cannot get down. This already happened yesterday."
Message 2 — Email · Government Building
"Can someone fix the front door of the Ministry Annex on Rua da Prata? It does not close properly and anyone can enter the lobby without badging in."
Message 3 — Email · Residential Building
"Hi, there is water coming through the ceiling in apartment 3C. I think it started after the upstairs neighbour used the washing machine. It's not urgent, I put a bucket under it."
Message 4 — WhatsApp · Residential Building
"The light in garage level -2 has been flickering for weeks."

Example Email Exchange (Supplier & Tenant)

System → Supplier

"Dear Team, we have a plumbing issue at Building 4, Apartment 3C. The tenant has reported water coming through the ceiling. Please confirm your earliest available slot for an inspection."

Supplier → System

"We can do Thursday afternoon between 14:00–17:00. Please confirm with the tenant."

System → Tenant

"Dear Resident of Apartment 3C, we have scheduled a plumbing inspection for Thursday between 14:00 and 17:00. Please ensure someone is available to provide access. We will follow up once the work is complete."
